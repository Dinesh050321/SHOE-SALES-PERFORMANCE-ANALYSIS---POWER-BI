# SHOE-SALES-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS - POWER-BI

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sports footwear sales data. The dashboard provides insights into overall sales performance, product trends, customer demographics, and payment behavior to support data-driven decision making.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Transformation
- Data Visualization

## Dataset Features
The dataset includes the following key attributes:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Model ID
- Gender
- Final Price
- Base Price
- Discount percentage
- Brand
- Product Category
- Size
- Color
- Sales Channel (Online / Retail)
- Payment Method
- Units Sold
- Customer Country
- Customer Income Level
- Customer Rating

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview Dashboard
Key KPIs and overall sales performance.

Metrics included:
- Total Revenue
- Total Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Online vs Offline Orders
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Discount

Visualizations:
- Orders Trend Over Time
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Units Sold by Sales Channel
- Payment Method Distribution

### 2. Product Analysis
Product performance and brand comparison.

Visualizations:
- Brand Wise Revenue
- Brand Growth Trend
- Category Wise Revenue
- Size Wise Sales
- Category Wise Units Sold
- Color Wise Sales
- Brand Wise Orders

### 3. Customer Analysis
Customer demographic insights.

Visualizations:
- Customer Income Distribution
- Customers by Gender
- Customers by Country (Map)
- Customer Rating by Brand
- Average Rating by Gender

## Key Insights
- Sports footwear sales show consistent performance across multiple brands.
- Online and retail channels contribute almost equally to total sales.
- Certain product categories generate higher revenue compared to others.
- Customer ratings remain generally high across brands.
- Revenue distribution varies across geographic regions.

## Project Outcome
The dashboard enables businesses to monitor sales performance, analyze customer behavior, and evaluate product performance through interactive visualizations, helping support strategic business decisions.
